Privacy Policy

This policy describes how Nilora processes information when you use the mood journal app.

Effective 14 September 2026

Who operates Nilora

Nilora is developed and operated by Muthamilselvan V. Privacy questions and data requests can be sent to muthamilselvan251@gmail.com.

Registered account information

When you register, Nilora uses Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore to process your email address, Firebase account identifier, display name, profile biography, and account timestamps. Your password is handled by Firebase Authentication and is not stored in Nilora’s local database.

Mood and journal information

For signed-in users, mood, weather, selected activities, mood intensity, journal title and notes, date, and favourite status are stored in Cloud Firestore and cached in a local SQLite database. Nilora uses this information to provide journal history, search, favourites, statistics, trends, and restoration.

Mood and journal entries may contain sensitive personal or wellbeing information that you choose to write. Only enter information you are comfortable storing in the app.

Photos

Profile and journal image files remain on the device in the current version and are not uploaded. They are not restored after uninstalling Nilora or changing devices.

Guest mode

Guest mood and journal entries are stored only in the local SQLite database on the device and are not sent to Firebase. Guest data is not restored after uninstalling Nilora or clearing its app storage.

Camera and photo selection

Nilora requests Camera access only after you choose to take a photo. On Android, gallery selection uses the system photo picker and does not request broad photo-library access. On iOS, Photo Library access is requested when you choose a gallery image.

Service providers and sharing

Nilora uses Google Firebase for authentication and cloud database storage. Information is sent to Firebase only as needed to provide those features. Nilora does not sell personal data. If you use Share, you choose the receiving app and the information to share.

Security and retention

Cloud communication uses encrypted network connections, and local information is stored in Nilora’s app storage. No storage method is completely secure. Account and journal information is retained while the account is active or until deletion is requested, except where retention is legally required.

Deletion and your choices

You can delete individual journal entries in Nilora. To permanently delete your registered account and associated cloud and local data, use Delete Account in the Profile section or follow the account-deletion instructions. Guest data can be removed by clearing app storage or uninstalling Nilora.

Policy updates

This policy may be updated when Nilora’s features or data practices change. The effective date identifies the current version.
