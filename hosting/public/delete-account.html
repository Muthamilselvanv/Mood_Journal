Account and data

Delete your Nilora account

You can permanently delete your account inside Nilora or request help when you cannot access the app.

Delete inside the app

  1. Sign in to your Nilora account.
  2. Open Profile.
  3. Open Account & Data.
  4. Select Delete my account.
  5. Enter your current password and confirm permanent deletion.

Request deletion without app access

Email Nilora support from the address registered to your Nilora account. Use the subject “Nilora account deletion request.” We will use the registered address and additional verification steps when necessary to protect the account from unauthorized deletion.

Request account deletion

Information deleted

  • Your Firebase Authentication account.
  • Your Nilora profile record, including name, email reference, biography, and account identifier.
  • Your locally stored profile photo on the device where in-app deletion is completed.
  • Your cloud mood and journal entries.
  • Your locally cached Nilora account and journal information on the device where in-app deletion is completed.

This action is permanent. Deleted account data cannot be restored. Limited records may be retained only where required by law, security, fraud prevention, or dispute resolution.

Guest data

Guest data is stored locally and is not connected to a registered cloud account. Remove it by clearing Nilora’s app storage or uninstalling the app.
